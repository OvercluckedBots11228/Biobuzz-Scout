Only show teams that…

A team counts if it did it in at least half of its scouted matches.

…and were tagged with

A tag counts if any scout tapped it in any match.

Pick list

Plan alliance selection. Star teams on the Robots screen or here, sort them into tiers, and use the arrows to put each tier in order. Saved on this phone, works offline.

Official results

When you have internet, download the event's official scores and rankings from FTCScout. They're saved on this phone and shown beside your scouting, so you can spot scouts who record results or ranking points differently from the referees.

Lead data

Everything the lead has scanned lives only on this device. Save a copy every few matches.

All reports

Find any report to correct it (match number, team, position, scout or any count) or delete it. Search by match number, team number, team name or scout.

    Second lead phone

    Copy everything on this phone (reports, deletions and pick lists) to another lead phone, for a backup or to hand over the lead role. On the other phone: unlock Lead, open Scan, start the camera, and point it here. The codes change by themselves; keep scanning until it says every part is in.

    Change the lead PIN

    Type a new PIN to get a line for config.js. Paste it over the leadPinHash line there and upload the file. Every phone then needs the new PIN.
